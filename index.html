Clarity turns a city's cameras into one coordinated search. The alert doesn't broadcast. It travels the camera network itself, expanding only as fast as a person could move, handing context from one camera to the next until someone is found.

The problem
Thefirst houriseverything
01

Alerts go out, not outward

An alert is broadcast to everyone at once, or drawn as a radius on a map. Nobody moves in a circle. People move along streets, transit lines and sidewalks, so a radius searches mostly ground they could never have reached.

02

Cameras never talk to each other

Every feed is watched alone. The camera two blocks north has no idea what the one behind the station just saw, so the same person is found from scratch, over and over.

03

The area grows as time squared

Someone on foot is inside 0.8 mi² after ten minutes and 7 mi² after thirty. Every minute spent organising costs more than the minute before it.

The alert is
a walker.

It spawns at the point last seen and expands only along edges it could have covered in the elapsed time. A camera nine minutes out isn't watching yet. It starts at t+9.

# the alert is not a message. it is a walker. walker Bolo { has description: str; has origin_ts: int; has trail: list; can hunt with Camera entry { hit = watch(here.feed, self.description, self.trail); if hit.matched { self.trail.append(here); self.origin_ts = hit.ts; # re-bloom } visit [-->](?travel_s <= elapsed(self.origin_ts)); } }

On a hit,
it re-blooms.

The walker relocates to that camera, resets its clock and expands again from the new position. The confirmed path is written back into the graph. On a full miss, confidence decays and the search retires itself.

Built to be questioned
Nofaces.Ever.
01
Descriptions, not identities
The network only ever knows "red jacket, blue duffel". No face embeddings, no enrolment database, nothing that can become a watchlist later. It cannot recognise anyone it wasn't told to look for.
02
Searches expire on their own
Confidence decays with every frontier that comes back empty. A search that stops finding anything retires itself rather than running quietly forever.
03
Every search leaves a trail
The walker writes its own path into the graph, so there is a permanent record of which cameras were activated, when, and why. Auditable by construction, not by policy.
